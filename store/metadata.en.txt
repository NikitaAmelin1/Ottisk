OTTISK: Living Abyss

Short description (80):
Hold your creature, gather light, and survive hunters in a living abyss.

Full description:
The creature lives only under your finger.

OTTISK is an atmospheric one-touch survival arcade. Hold to keep your creature alive, guide it to light sources, and change course when hunters emerge from the dark.

Every run becomes a tense dance:
• collect light and leave a trail in the abyss;
• unlock heroes and choose mutations;
• face the Leviathan and the multi-phase Kraken;
• join the daily run and weekly challenges;
• earn trophies and improve your best score.

Easy to learn, hard to master. How far can you go without letting go?
